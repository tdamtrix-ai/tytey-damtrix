01 / KARIADUDU STREET STYLE
01
STREETS & PRAYERS

Streets & Prayers ndio intro yangu kwa game. Niliandika hii nikiwa kwa rooftop Kariadudu nikiangalia Nairobi yote. Ni maisha ya kila siku ya msee wa mtaa - unaamka asubuhi, mfuko empty, lakini bado unaimani. Beat ni boom trap mzito na drums zinagonga kifua, na flow yangu inachanganya Swahili na Sheng ya mtaa. Kila bar inatoka kwa roho, sio ya kucopy. Nabonga juu ya mama yangu kuniprayia, mabratha kunisupport, na streets kunifundisha hustle. Hii wimbo unafaa kusikiza ukiwa kwa matatu, kwa job, ama ukijipanga. Ni prayer na hustle kwa pamoja. Resilience, hope, na real life - zote kwa track moja. Hii ndio sound ya Eastlands.

02 / BABA DOGO ORIGINS
02
NAIROBI NIGHTS

Nairobi Nights ni anthem ya usiku. Kupitia Baba Dogo streets hadi CBD lights, tunapanda pamoja. Cold night, warm cypher, mic iko hot. From Kariadudu base mpaka kanjo street cyphers, we rep where we come from. Hooks zimejawa story za hustle, sacrifice, na ambition. Beat ni dark, 808s heavy, vibe ni cinematic.

03
SWAHILI FLOW

Swahili flow ni roho yangu. Mixing Sheng, Swahili, and English to tell real stories. Bars zimejaa culture, pride, na struggle. From estate life to studio lights, keeping it authentic. No gimmicks, just raw flow, real lyrics. Supporting young talent in the mtaa, building the culture one verse at a time.
